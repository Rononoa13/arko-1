Arko 1 is a one-phone party experience for tracking drinks and playing games together.

What is Arko 1?

Whether you're hanging out with friends, starting a game night, or looking for something fun to do together, Arko 1 brings everyone into the same experience.

No accounts. No complicated setup. Just one phone and a group ready to play.

How it works

Made for sharing

Not every group activity needs an app installed on everyone's phone.

One person opens Arko 1, puts the phone in the middle, and everyone joins in. The experience is designed to be straightforward, accessible, and easy to share.

Less setup. More interaction.
